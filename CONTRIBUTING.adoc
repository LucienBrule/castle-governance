= Contributing / Source Style

== Normative text rules

. Do not auto-wrap or reflow legal prose.
. Prefer one operative paragraph or clause per source line.
. Preserve defined-term capitalization.
. Every Article and Section in structured bylaws must have a stable explicit anchor.
. Formatting-only changes must not churn normative prose.
. Do not silently "correct" a transcription. If the PDF appears erroneous, preserve the source text and raise the issue separately.
. The source PDF remains authoritative for transcription disputes.

== Change discipline

Governance proposals should be atomic and reviewable.
Commit messages should state the problem, the proposed change, and the reason for the change.
Do not mix unrelated policy changes into one commit merely because they touch the same Article.

== OCR / provenance

Raw OCR belongs under `ocr/<document>/page-NNN.txt`.
The structured AsciiDoc may correct OCR errors only where the rendered source artifact or reliable embedded text makes the correction unambiguous.
Source hashes must pass before a patch is considered reviewable.
