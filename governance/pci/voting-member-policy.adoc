The following policy was adopted by the PCI board at the 14 September 2026 Board Meeting:

To be considered a Voting Member, Alumni Members must satisfy one of the following criteria: Make a donation or pay dues to PCI of at least $25 in the last 12 months but two weeks prior to the meeting. Make a donation of at least $25 to PCF in the last 12 months but two weeks prior to the meeting. Purchase a ticket and attend the Reunion/PCI Annual Meeting. Become an Alumni Member in the last 12 months. Obtain a waiver of these requirements which is approved by a majority of PCI directors.
